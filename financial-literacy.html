Financial Literacy for Life

Adaptive Financial Education for Individuals with Special Needs

Empowering individuals with IDD, autism, neurodiversity, and learning differences to build real-world financial skills and independence.

Educational purposes only. This program does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice, and Hug Your Head Foundation, INC. is not a licensed financial advisor.

About the Program

Financial Literacy for Life is an adaptive financial education program designed specifically for individuals with special needs, including intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD), autism, neurodiversity, and learning differences. The program delivers accessible, hands-on instruction using visual supports, repetition, guided practice, and real-life simulations to ensure comprehension and retention. Programs are delivered in small-group settings through partnerships with schools, transition programs, and disability service organizations.

Core Skills We Teach

💰

Budgeting & Money Management

Practical daily budgeting skills and hands-on money management for real-life independence.

🏦

Banking & Digital Tools

Safe use of banks, debit cards, and digital payment tools with guided practice.

🎯

Saving & Goal Setting

Building healthy saving habits and setting achievable financial goals.

🛒

Spending Decisions

Understanding needs vs. wants and making confident, informed spending choices.

🚨

Scam Prevention

Recognizing financial scams and protecting against exploitation — a critical life skill.

🔄

Financial Routines

Building consistent routines that support long-term financial independence.

Who This Program Serves

🧠

Individuals with IDD

Adults and young adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities learning to manage money independently.

🌟

Autism Spectrum

Individuals on the autism spectrum who benefit from structured, visual, and repetitive learning approaches.

📚

Neurodiverse Learners

Students and adults with neurodiversity including ADHD, dyslexia, and other learning differences.

🎓

Transition Programs

High school and post-secondary transition programs preparing students for financial independence.
